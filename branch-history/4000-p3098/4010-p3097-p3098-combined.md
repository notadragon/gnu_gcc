## Rationale

Regression tests for P3097 (virtual functions) and P3098 (postcondition
captures) exercised together, pinned as their own commit rather than folded
into either paper's.  Neither `3000-p3097` nor `4000-p3098` can carry them:
each paper's own commit only has the other paper's flag off, so a test that
needs both -- a capturing postcondition on a *virtual* function, or a
re-entrant instantiation that crosses both features at once -- cannot exist
until both are in the tree, and by the time both are, attributing the test
to one paper over the other would be arbitrary.

`contract-reentrancy-p3097-p3098.C` is the direct sequel to
`3000-p3097`'s own `contract-reentrancy-p3097.C` and `4000-p3098`'s
`contract-reentrancy-p3098.C`: a virtual function with a capturing
postcondition, reached re-entrantly from inside another contract's
predicate, is the densest combination the two features allow together --
both the vtable-dispatch wrapper and the capture-struct machinery are live
at once, before the outer predicate has finished substituting.
`contract-reentrancy-run.C` is the run-time counterpart to every compile-only
reentrancy test in this history: a predicate that silently never runs, or
substitutes against the wrong object, compiles just as quietly as a correct
one, so this checks that the re-entrantly instantiated contracts actually
evaluate, in order, against the right objects, for a plain, a virtual and a
capturing callee alike.  `matrix-throwpaths-p3850-run.C` is a
`contract-matrix-gen.py`-generated matrix over every way a contract
assertion can materialize and throw -- precondition, postcondition, a
result binding, `contract_assert`, a P3098 capture initializer, a P3098
capturing predicate, and a P3097 interface contract in the virtual wrapper --
at `-O0` and `-O2`; despite compiling with the umbrella `-fcontracts-p3850`,
every materialization it actually covers is P3097 or P3098, which is why it
sits here rather than in a later, broader commit.
`noexceptions-postcondition-capture.C` checks that a capturing postcondition
still compiles and runs under `-fno-exceptions`, a P3098-only concern that
simply did not exist before this pairing was possible to write against.

## Compile gap

None -- it adds only tests, and every feature it exercises is already
present by this point: P3097 and P3098 are both direct dependencies, and
`matrix-throwpaths-p3850-run.C`'s broader `-fcontracts-p3850` flag does not
smuggle in anything from a later paper -- its "Materialisations covered"
list is precondition, postcondition, a result binding, `contract_assert`,
a P3098 capture initializer, a P3098 capturing predicate, a P3097 interface
contract, and a constructor/destructor, none of which need P3099, P3400,
P4283 or any paper after this one in the linear history.
