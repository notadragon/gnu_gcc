## Rationale

What is left of the P2900 band once every fix that can be attributed to a bug
has been given its own commit (`1010` through `1210`).  This is deliberately
the residue, and after the test coverage moved out it is of one kind.

**Base-facility hunks that a later paper's selectors cannot claim.**  Several
functions in `contracts.cc` were assigned whole to this band, and parts of
their interiors belong to papers that come later: the capture-struct reference
parameters and the `__pre_fn`-handles-capture-init restructuring in
`build_contract_condition_function` and `apply_postconditions` (P3098), the
`processing_postcondition` to `processing_postcondition_predicate` rename and
the identity registration of captures in `rebuild_postconditions` (P3098), the
`compute_comment` facet application in `update_late_contract` and the
`STRING_CST` extraction helper above it (P3400), and the
`all_contracts_statically_nonthrowing` arm of the noexcept decision (P4298).
None of them is a bug fix, so none belongs in one of the per-bug commits; each
would ideally be claimed by its own paper, which would mean rewriting those
entries' whole-function selectors into per-hunk ones.  Recorded here rather
than moved.

**It holds no tests, deliberately.**  An entry named `p2900-fixes`,
sequenced ahead of every fix and containing no fixes, is the wrong home for
1,700 lines of tests -- several of which exercise behaviour those later
commits establish.  The standard-wording citations, the generated
cross-product matrices and the behaviour baselines live in
`1900-p2900-expanded-test-coverage` at the end of the band, where they
demonstrate the milestone rather than precede it.

## Compile gap

This commit is the one place in the band where a compile gap is intrinsic
rather than incidental: several of its hunks name things no earlier commit
declares.  Standing alone it would have to carry stubs for
`active_postcondition_with_captures_p`, `get_postcondition_capture_struct_type`,
`has_postcondition_captures_p`, `POSTCONDITION_CAPTURES` and
`postcondition_capture_flags` (all replaced by `4000-p3098`),
`apply_label_string_facet` and `CONTRACT_LABEL` (replaced by
`4200-p3400-core`), and `flag_contracts_p4298` with
`all_contracts_statically_nonthrowing` (replaced by `4800-p4298`).  The
`processing_postcondition_predicate` rename likewise needs the old spelling
kept until `4000-p3098` renames the variable for real.
